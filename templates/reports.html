2. Important Findings

Non-linear Superiority

Tree-based ensembles (Gradient Boosting, Random Forest) consistently outperformed linear baselines, demonstrating non-linear interactions in claim risk factors.

Unsupervised Limitations

Mathematical clusters (KMeans, DBSCAN) did not provide additional lift when concatenated as features, confirming unsupervised clusters do not inherently capture insurance risk variance.

Statistical Significance

Paired bootstrap testing verified Gradient Boosting's MAE superiority over Ridge baseline with empirical p = 0.014 (< 0.05).
