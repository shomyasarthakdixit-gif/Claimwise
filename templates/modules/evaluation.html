1. Three-Way Split Discipline

Topic 5.1

Core Principle: The hold-out test set must be strictly locked and inaccessible during feature selection, imputation, and hyperparameter tuning.

Why Hold-Out Is Essential: Peeking at test data to adjust learning rates, tree depth, or feature sets introduces Data Snooping Bias. The test score becomes overly optimistic and fails to generalize to real insurance claims.

2. k-Fold & Nested Cross-Validation

Topic 5.2

Nested CV Architecture: Decouples model parameter optimization (inner loop) from unbiased generalization evaluation (outer loop).

Stratified k-Fold: Bins continuous claim amounts into severity tiers to guarantee heavy catastrophic losses are distributed identically across every split fold.

3. Classification Metrics & Imbalance

Topic 5.3

Accuracy and When It Lies: On imbalanced claim data (85% standard, 15% severe), a dummy model predicts all standard, scoring 85% accuracy with 0% recall!

ROC-AUC vs PR-AUC: Severe class imbalance causes True Negatives to artificially inflate ROC-AUC. PR-AUC focuses directly on true positive precision and recall.

4. Regression Metrics & Outlier Sensitivity

Topic 5.4

RMSE vs MAE Sensitivity: Quadratic L2 penalty vs robust linear L1 median penalty under heavy-tailed insurance claim shocks.

Metric Formula Outlier Penalty Recommended Use
RMSE √(Σ(y - ŷ)² / n) Quadratic ($L_2$) When extreme errors threaten solvency
MAE Σ|y - ŷ| / n Linear ($L_1$) Robust baseline for heavy-tailed claims
MAPE 100 × Σ|(y - ŷ)/y| / n Relative % Intuitive percentage reporting
R² 1 - SS_res / SS_tot Variance Ratio Variance explained vs sample mean

5. Probability Calibration

Topic 5.5

Why 0.7 Must Mean 70%: In underwriting, Expected Claim Cost = P(Claim) × Loss. Uncalibrated probabilities lead directly to mispriced policies.

  • Platt Scaling (Sigmoid Method) Parametric logistic regression mapping decision scores to well-calibrated probabilities.
  • Isotonic Regression (PAVA) Non-parametric piecewise constant isotonic step function achieving the lowest Brier error (0.0925).

6. Hyperparameter Search Strategies

Topic 5.6

Bergstra-Bengio (2012) Theorem: Random Search explores more distinct coordinates than Grid Search on the effective dimensions.

  • Bayesian Optimization (Gaussian Processes Preview) Probabilistic surrogate model balancing Upper Confidence Bound exploration and peak exploitation.

7. Learning & Validation Curves

Topic 5.7

Diagnosing Model Health: Identifying under-fitting (high bias), over-fitting (high variance), and data scarcity from a single diagnostic chart.

Diagnosis on ClaimWise: As training examples grow to 10,000, training R² converges near 0.53 and cross-validation converges to 0.49, demonstrating stable convergence without pathological high-variance overfit.

8. Statistical Significance Testing

Topic 5.8

Beyond Nominal Metrics: Proving that Gradient Boosting's lead over Ridge baseline is statistically significant and not stochastic noise.

Paired Bootstrap Result: 1,000 resamples yield a 95% Confidence Interval strictly positive [+$8.65, +$68.69] with empirical p = 0.014 (< 0.05). We reject the null hypothesis of equal performance.
